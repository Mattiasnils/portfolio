About

I design coherent system experiences across hardware and software platforms

I'm a designer and engineer specialized in design systems and ecosystem experience. I care about how products feel as a connected whole — shared patterns, coherent interaction models, and interfaces that stay clear as platforms grow in scale and complexity.

My work sits between design and engineering: building systems teams can reuse, shaping platform UX that stays consistent across surfaces, and grounding decisions in research so complexity resolves into simple and delightful experiences for users.

As a person, I'm curious, optimistic, initiative-taking, and at my best in teams that challenge assumptions and build with diverse perspectives.
