Ziklo Bank

Redesign of the mobile banking app

Summary

Redesigned the consumer-facing banking app CarPay with a focus on user experience, accessibility and cross-platform scalability.

Collaborated closely with the product owner and frontend developers to define product strategy and platform architecture while delivering conceptual, high-fidelity prototypes in Figma. Established the foundations to a design system featuring typography, colors, grids and responsive, token-based components. Researched and developed multi-platform design patterns to ensure a consistent, responsive and WCAG-compliant user experience across all devices.
